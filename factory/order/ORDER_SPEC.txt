ORDER SPEC v1 - what the brain (third model) delivers to the factory for one film
=====================================================================================================================
Principle: everything that needs THINKING is in the order (the brain decides WHAT); everything MECHANICAL is done by
the factory (exact times from the voice, camera keys, positions, sizes, colours, sides, rhythm, sound, checks - HOW).
The brain NEVER writes seconds, pixels, zoom values, colours of elements or code.

Folder: factory/orders/<name>/   (one folder = one film; example: factory/orders/ussr15 = our film v5 as an order)
  order.json        title, countries, chapters + music, opening, era names, places, capitals, datasets, end card
  narration.json    {"timing": {...}, "paragraphs": [{"id": "01", "text": "..."}]}  word for word = script_final.txt
  script_final.txt  the user's corrected script (English), for the record
  vo/vo_<id>.mp3    one platform take per paragraph (made in the chat, same voice_id)
  storyboard.json   the brain's decisions for every paragraph (below)
  sources.json      the brain's picks: archival photos (Wikimedia Commons file names), document texts, signatures,
                    extra flags (below). The processing station fetches, checks, processes and credits them.
  assets.json       3D clips, icons and music the factory already has (until the phase 3 workshop)
  data/             written by the factory (timeline.json from the voice job, tile indexes)

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- order.json
{"title": "...", "slug": "...",
 "countries": ["EST", "LVA", ...],            the film's countries (ISO3 = geo.json republics): tints, flags, borders
 "flags": {"SU1": "su"},                       only extra flag keys (ISO3 -> flag code is known to the factory)
 "places": {"Name": [lon, lat] | "rep:EST" | ["rep:GEO", "rep:ARM", "Tbilisi"]},   the brain's gazetteer (facts)
 "hook": {"paragraphs": ["01"], "music": "c1_hook"},
 "chapters": [{"n": "I", "title": "The Nesting Doll", "paragraphs": ["02"], "music": "c2_origins"}, ...,
              {"n": null, "title": "Conclusion", "paragraphs": ["07"], "music": "c5_resolution", "card": false}],
 "opening": {"clip": "<clip id or null>", "text_out": "words", "map_in": "words",
             "lines": [{"text": "25 DECEMBER", "cue": "December", "kind": "kicker"}, {"text": "1991", "cue": "1991", "kind": "year"},
                       {"text": "Moscow · the Kremlin", "kind": "place"}]},
 "names": {"sets": {"modern": {"rep:EST": "ESTONIA"}, "soviet": {"rep:EST": "ESTONIAN SSR", "shape:kfssr": "KARELO-FINNISH SSR"}},
           "eras": [{"from": "start", "set": "modern", "neighbours": true}, {"from": "02:start", "set": "soviet"},
                    {"from": "05:fifteen independent states", "set": "modern", "neighbours": true}],
           "neighbours": {"FIN": "FINLAND"}},
 "capitals": {"EST": "Tallinn"}, "datasets": {"<id>": [...]}, "end": {"text": "FILM TITLE", "sub": "optional"}}
Music cues (house score): c1_hook, c2_origins, c3_annex, c4_collapse, c5_resolution. Same cue on consecutive chapters
= one continuous bed. Places: geo.json ids (rep:/rr:/brk:/shape:/ctx:/river:), geo.json cities, or the gazetteer.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- storyboard.json
{"paragraphs": [{"id": "02", "beats": [ {beat}, {beat}, ... ]}, ...]}
A beat = "cue" (EXACT words of that paragraph) + one or more actions + options. Cue forms:
  "words"  |  "words#2" (2nd occurrence)  |  "start" / "end" (of the paragraph)  |  "03:words" (another paragraph)
"until" (optional) = "words" | "paragraph" | "chapter" | "end" (end of film) | "shot" (next camera move).
Without "until" the factory decides (map notes leave with the next camera move, cards stay readable 2.5-7.5 s,
fills/flags are map STATE and stay until the chapter ends or a new state replaces them).

CAMERA
  {"cue": "...", "go": "place" | ["place", ...] | "overview", "shot": "close | region | context"}   (optional shot)
  Chapters open wide automatically; holds over 10 s and gaps over 4 s get slow drifts; no framing repeats in 60 s;
  a "go" under a full-frame clip becomes a cut. {"camera": "hold"} on a go beat = no drifts on that shot.
MAP STATE
  {"fill": "rep:EST" | "film" | ["EST", "rr:Tatarstan", "group name"], "look": "...", "like": "UKR", "op": 0.55, "except": [...]}
     looks (state): tint (default), born (tint + light-traced border + pop), state, faint, muted, quilt, "#hex"
     looks (on top for a while): focus, highlight, gain, war, boycott, disputed, new
  {"unfill": ids}
  {"border": ids | "film" | "frontier", "style": "trace | plain | gold | fire | dashed | light | river"}
  {"flag": "EST" | ["GEO", "EST"] | "film", "date": "11 Mar 1990" | "dates": {"GEO": "9 Apr 1991"}, "at": "place", "glide": "words"}
  {"flags": "hide", "until": "end"}
MAP NOTES
  {"label": "TEXT", "at": "place", "sub": "...", "style": "region | water | italic | title | small", "color": "#hex"}
  {"marker": "city", "label": "Text", "sub": "...", "side": "r|l|t|b", "pulse": true, "alert": true, "dot": true}
  {"banner": "<photo id>", "title": "TATARSTAN", "sub": "...", "near": "place"}          photo on a pole
  {"icon": "<icon id>", "near": "place"}                                                  small 3D object
  {"arrow": ["A", "B"] | [["A", "B"], ["A", "C"]], "kind": "force | link"}
  {"route": ["A", "B", "C"], "style": "chain | converge | path"}    {"rings": "place"}
  {"dots": "<dataset>", "layers": [0, 1], "tempo": "slow | fast | all"}
SCREEN
  {"date": "30 DECEMBER 1922", "sub": "..."}   {"numeral": "90%+", "sub": "..."}   {"tag": "TEXT", "num": "I"} (or slots)
  {"quote": "“...”", "sub": "attribution"}   {"strike": ["LANGUAGE", "RELIGION"]}   {"callout": "TEXT"}   {"big": "TEXT"}
  {"portrait": "<photo id>", "name": "...", "sub": "..."}          (a photo with "use": "portrait")
  {"document": "<photo id | document id>", "caption": "...", "signatures": [{"img": "<signature id>", "name": "..."}]}
  {"titles": [{"text": "...", "cue": "...", "hi": "15", "hi_cue": "..."}]}       the hook title sequence
  {"closing": "EXACTLY 15 COUNTRIES"}                                            closing title + end card + credits
3D / CREATIVE
  {"clip": "<clip id>", "steps": [{"cue": "...", "seg": "<segment>"}], "labels": [{"text": "...", "k": 0, "cue": "..."}]}
  {"creative": "star_shatter | lights_out | chain", "params": {...}}   (phase 3 adds the toolbox)
  {"accent": "war | tension | crowd | bell | seal | boom | swell | forge | count | finale | riser | shimmer | wind | paper | draw"}

The factory refuses an order with a clear message when a cue is not in the narration, a place or asset is unknown, or a
key is not in this list. Chapter cards, whooshes, flag drops, paper/stamp/tick sounds, music cross-fades, era names,
credits and the overlap/off-screen fixes are automatic.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- sources.json
The brain chooses (it knows the story, the event and the era); the PROCESSING STATION does the rest.
{"photos": {
   "kazan":     {"commons": "Казанский кремль. Панорама с колеса обозрения.jpg", "use": "banner", "what": "Kazan Kremlin"},
   "lenin":     {"commons": "Lenin in 1920 (cropped).jpg", "use": "portrait", "what": "Lenin (1920)"},
   "const1936": {"commons": "The Soviet Socialist Constitution of december 5, 1936.djvu", "page": 3, "use": "document", "what": "..."},
   "belovezha": {"commons": "RIAN archive 848095 ....jpg", "use": ["document", "scene"], "what": "...", "author": "optional fix"}},
 "documents": {
   "article72": {"layout": "article", "head": "CONSTITUTION OF THE USSR · 1977", "title": "Article 72",
                 "body": ["Each Union Republic shall retain", "the right freely to secede", "from the USSR."], "underline": 1},
   "uti":       {"layout": "maxim", "title": "Uti possidetis juris", "gloss": ["new states inherit the borders", "..."], "seal": "1991"}},
 "signatures": {"yeltsin": {"commons": "Yeltsin signature.svg", "what": "Signature of Boris Yeltsin"}},
 "flags": {"su": {"commons": "Flag of the Soviet Union.svg"}},          only flags that are not a modern ISO flag
 "credits_fixed": ["..."]}                                               optional, else the house lines (relief, imagery, ...)
use: banner (photo on a pole, 2.5D depth) | scene (2.5D photo) | portrait (cut-out) | document (page / paper photo).
"commons" = the exact file name on Wikimedia Commons; "page" for djvu / pdf / tif pages; "what" = short credit caption.
Documents: up to 6 body lines of at most 44 characters; title_font "serif" | "script"; seal = a short date.
The station REFUSES (ORDER ERROR, all problems listed at once): a file that does not exist, a licence other than
Public domain / CC0 / CC BY / CC BY-SA (no NC, no ND, no fair use), an unknown key, a portrait with no person in it.
Automatic: download in the best size, scan-border trim, 4x super-resolution of small files, levels, depth + near layer,
focus point (faces) so no crop cuts the subject, cut-outs, Manim documents, credits of what the film really shows
(end card + CREDITS.txt for the YouTube description) and review sheets (original | processed | depth | cut-out).
